## Premise

Protagonist **王动 (Wang Dong)** is reborn/regressed roughly 20 years into his own
past, carrying an assistant system with three abilities:
- **扫描 (Scan)** — reveals a warship's true growth potential/aptitude before
  committing resources to it.
- **源点获取翻倍 (Origin-Point Acquisition Multiplier)** — lets a ship gain origin
  points (the novel's core upgrade currency) at 3x, 5x, up to 100x the normal rate.
- **模块特性自选 (Module Trait Self-Select)** — turns a normally-random module trait
  roll into a free choice.

This is a striking near-1:1 match for Emberwake's existing systems (Scan, Yield/
Origin Essence multiplier framing, Lock/trait-selection) — those were evidently
already built with this premise in mind, even before this research pass.

The novel's own term for this class of ship is **火种战舰 ("Spark/Ember-type
warship")** — 火种 (huǒzhǒng) literally means "spark," "ember," or "tinder-seed."
This is very likely where this project's own name ("Emberwake") and its AI
companion's name ("the Cinder") originated. Worth flagging as a real, confirmed
resonance rather than coincidence — future story work should lean into it rather
than treat it as a naming accident.

His starting ship in the comic adaptation is named **战斧号 ("Tomahawk"/"Battle-Axe",
literally "War-Axe-号")**. A different search result named a later/upgraded flagship
幽鹏号 ("Dark Roc"/"Shadow Roc") — likely a subsequent hull, not the starting one
(sources disagree or refer to different points in his ship progression; not fully
resolved, flagged for follow-up if later acts need it).

## Key figures found

- **王动 (Wang Dong)** — protagonist, reborn ~20 years early, holds noble rank
  **男爵 (Baron)**.
- **莉雅 (Liya)** — secondary character, named in early searches; specific role not
  yet confirmed (not enough source detail surfaced this pass — flagged for follow-up,
  not load-bearing for Act I as currently scoped).
- **黑石男爵 / "Baron Blackstone" (黑石 = "Black Stone")** — named antagonist,
  introduced comic ch. 26 ("黑石男爵"), also referenced in novel-chapter search as
  ch. 28 ("黑石男爵").
- **白牙星盗团 ("White Fang Star Pirates")**, shortened in the comic's chapter titles
  to **牙星盗团 ("Fang Star Pirates")** — the recurring antagonist raider faction.
  Novel-chapter search places their first appearance at ch. 25 ("白牙星盗团！").
- **天虎商队 ("Sky Tiger Trade Caravan")** — a trade-convoy faction whose defeat is a
  named beat (comic ch. 17, "天虎商队的落幕" / "Tiger Caravan's Downfall").

## Locations / structure

- **高地星 (Highland Star)** — a territory Wang Dong secures (novel-chapter search:
  ch. 84, "高地星到手" / "Highland Star Secured" — later than Act I's likely scope).
- **北地 (North Territory)** — a broader region, also the title of volume 2
  ("北地风起" / "North Territory: Wind Rises").
- **男爵领 ("the Baron's territory/domain")** — a feudal-fief structure underpinning
  his noble rank; referenced directly in comic ch. 20's title ("回归男爵领前的隐患" /
  "Hidden Dangers Before Returning to the Baron's Domain").
- Novel volumes (per Baidu Baike, current to ch. 2756+): **矿星 ("Mine Star")**,
  **北地风起 ("North Territory: Wind Rises")**, **新的启点 ("A New Starting Point")**
  — Act I's setting maps most directly onto the "矿星" (Mine Star) opening volume.

## Early beat sequence (comic adaptation, chapters 1–~28)

Numbered loosely by the comic's own episode order (titles translated by me, not
quoted from any translation source):

1. 時光回溯 — Time Rewind (the rebirth trigger)
2. 火種戰艦——戰斧號 — Spark Warship — *Tomahawk* (starting ship introduced)
3. 扭轉命運的時刻 — The Moment Fate Turns
4. 我，就是指揮官 — I Am the Commander (Wang Dong takes command)
5. 就這！菜雞！ — That's It? Weakling! (he's underestimated)
6. 初入戰場 — First Taste of Battle
7. 聚會 — A Gathering
8. 暗潮洶湧 — Undercurrents
9. 戰起 — Battle Begins
10. 勝利的天平 — The Scales of Victory
11. 形勢逆轉 — The Tide Turns
12. 黃雀在後 — The Mantis Stalks the Cicada (idiom: a hidden third party exploits
    two others' conflict)
13. 波瀾再起 — New Waves Rise
14. 意料之外的人 — An Unexpected Person
15. 礦場變故 — Incident at the Mine
16. 礦工們的反擊 — The Miners' Counterattack
17. 天虎商隊的落幕 — Tiger Caravan's Downfall
18. 聚會波瀾 — Disturbance at the Gathering
19. 戰場驚變 — Sudden Change on the Battlefield
20. 迴歸男爵領前的隱患 — Hidden Dangers Before Returning to the Baron's Domain
21. 血脈確認 — Bloodline Confirmation (his identity/legitimacy formally verified)
22. 別了，礦星 — Farewell, Mine Star (leaving the starting world)
23. 牙星盜團 — Fang Star Pirates (raider faction, named on-screen)
24. 前世的重現？ — A Recurrence of a Past Life? (a déjà-vu beat — something from his
    original, unlived future recurs early)
25. 新的起點 — A New Beginning (likely the volume-2 transition point)
26. 黑石男爵 — Baron Blackstone (named antagonist introduced)
27. 初次鍛體 — First Body Conditioning
28. 修行天才 — A Prodigy at Cultivation

Beats 27–28 point to a **personal cultivation/training progression** running
alongside the ship-upgrade system — Wang Dong's own body/power level grows, not just
his fleet. Emberwake has no equivalent system (crew/ship progression only). Noting
this as a real structural difference rather than silently ignoring it: out of scope
for Act I content grounding, but worth a design conversation if later acts want to
adapt the personal-growth thread (e.g., a Kade-specific skill/perk track).

## How this maps onto Emberwake's existing Act I (see act-1-bauhinia-reach.md)

Emberwake keeps its own original names (Kade Ren, the Cinder, Whisper, Bauhinia
Reach, House Arthaine, Shark Reavers, Tiger Shark) rather than relabeling to the
novel's cast — per `docs/world-bible.md`'s pre-existing house rule, this project
adapts beats into original prose/setting, it doesn't translate or reproduce the
source. The grounding work is about making the existing chapters' **plot beats**
traceable to specific novel beats above, not renaming characters. Per-chapter
mapping and the actual content changes are documented inline in
`act-1-bauhinia-reach.md` and in the `ACT1_SCENES` data comments in
`src/data/story/act1.ts`.

Confirmed resonances worth calling out:
- The "twenty years earlier" rebirth-into-command framing (existing Ch.1) already
  matches 時光回溯 / 我，就是指揮官 structurally — no change needed there, just
  documented as intentional.
- The existing Ch.4 "wreck is an echo of Kade's future flagship" beat independently
  arrived at essentially the same idea as 前世的重現 (a past-life/future echo
  recurring early) — also just documented, not rewritten.
- 礦場變故 (Incident at the Mine) has **no existing equivalent** in Ch.1, which is
  currently pure tutorial with no complication — this is a real gap, addressed by
  adding a mine-incident beat (see Ch.1 changes).
- 黃雀在後 (hidden third party exploiting a fight) has **no existing equivalent** in
  Ch.2's Reaver-raid beat — addressed by planting a seed/thread (see Ch.2 changes),
  not a full new faction reveal (too large a scope change for one pass).
- 血脈確認 (Bloodline Confirmation) maps onto Ch.3's Yifei Lin recognition beat,
  which was previously vague ("recognizing promise... left ambiguous") — sharpened
  into an explicit verification beat.
