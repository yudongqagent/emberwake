# Act V — Second Ignition (Umbral Line)

Endgame. Pays off every faction-choice thread from Acts II–IV, brings the campaign
full circle to Kade's opening death, and resolves with a branching epilogue rather than
a single fixed ending.

Galaxy: **Umbral Line** — one vast convergence zone rather than a graph of many
systems, reflecting the endgame's narrower, more focused staging.

**Novel grounding:** see `docs/story/research-notes-act5.md`. Honest result of that
research: the source novel is still ongoing serialization (3159+ chapters, updated as
recently as today) with no published finale — there is no endgame to adapt Act V's
beats from. This act is intentionally original invention rather than force-fit to a
weak match; the two loosest thematic cousins found (ch. 120 "文明吞噬"/Civilization
Devouring for a draining-antagonist concept, ch. 176 "复仇的终结"/The End of Revenge
for a full-circle payoff) are noted per-chapter below but not claimed as sourcing.

---

## Chapter 1 — Calling the Reach

**System:** Bauhinia Prime → touring prior galaxies

**Beats:**
- Kade has to rally a real coalition before confronting the Hollow — this chapter is
  structured as a tour through previously-unlocked galaxies, calling in every thread
  the player has built:
  - Bauhinia: strength of support depends on `arthaineResolution` from Act IV Ch.3.
  - Lionsheart/Swanreach: which faction(s) show up, and how enthusiastically, depends
    on `ridgeReachOutcome` from Act II Ch.5.
  - Shark Reavers: present and meaningfully helpful only if `tigerSharkAlliance` was
    set in Act III Ch.3 (and she's alive/recruited).
  - Chitin Swarm: a remnant faction may offer aid, framed as the Broodmother
    recognizing a shared enemy — availability depends on how Act III Ch.5 played out.
  - Mayeth Constructs: Requiem can rouse a small number of dormant Constructs to help,
    unconditionally (he's earned that much trust by now).
- The chapter's overall "coalition strength" is tallied from these flags and feeds
  directly into Ch.5's final battle composition and difficulty framing — not a hard
  fail state, but a real difference in how the finale feels and looks.

**Characters:** Kade, full crew, variable faction representatives.

**Choices & Consequences:** No new choices — this chapter reads prior ones.

**Systems/Unlocks:** None mechanical — narrative payoff chapter.

**Novel grounding:** no confirmed source — see `docs/story/research-notes-act5.md`.
A multi-faction coalition-rally is a common enough genre beat that it's plausible the
novel does something like it eventually, but no source confirms it, and the novel
hasn't reached a point where it could be checked. Kept as original invention.

## Chapter 2 — The Umbral Line

**System:** the Umbral Line (outer approach)

**Beats:**
- Journey to where the Hollow is gathering. Environmental and audio design lean hard
  into dread — the visual language here should feel like a controlled inversion of the
  holographic aesthetic used everywhere else (color drained, signal noise, UI elements
  that flicker as if the Hollow interferes with instrumentation).
- First direct Hollow combat encounter, small scale — establishes its doctrine:
  it doesn't just damage ships, it drains Origin-type resources directly, punishing
  players who over-invested in raw stockpiles rather than a balanced fleet.

**Characters:** Kade, the Cinder, current crew.

**Choices & Consequences:** None major.

**Systems/Unlocks:** None mechanical — Hollow enemy doctrine introduced.

**Novel grounding:** no confirmed source for the Hollow specifically — see
`docs/story/research-notes-act5.md`. Kept as original invention.

## Chapter 3 — Echoes of the Losing Battle

**System:** the Umbral Line (deep approach)

**Beats:**
- Kade re-encounters, almost beat-for-beat, the losing battle he died in at the very
  start of the game — same formation shape, same opening moves from the enemy,
  deliberately recognizable to a player who remembers Chapter 1's cold open.
- This time it plays out fully as a real, winnable combat encounter using the complete
  built-up fleet and crew, framed explicitly (in dialogue and presentation) as the
  moment the loop closes: the same fight, everything different about how Kade meets
  it.
- Thematic full-circle beat, minimal new mechanics — the point is emotional weight, not
  new systems.

**Characters:** Kade, the Cinder, full crew.

**Choices & Consequences:** None major.

**Systems/Unlocks:** None — pure narrative payoff.

**Novel grounding:** no confirmed source. Ch. 176's "复仇的终结" (The End of Revenge)
is a loose emotional cousin — a revenge-arc closure beat — but nothing confirms it's
a re-fight of the protagonist's original death/failure scenario specifically. Given
the novel's own rebirth premise, a full-circle rematch is plausible as something the
author is building toward, but plausible isn't sourced; kept as original invention.

## Chapter 4 — What Kade Knows

**System:** Bauhinia Prime (brief return) → Umbral Line staging

**Beats:**
- Before the final battle, a last major choice: with the political vacuum left by
  Arthur's fall (Act IV Ch.3) and everything Kade's built, what does he actually do
  with the standing he's earned?
- **Choice point:** (a) take up real institutional power within the Principality —
  reform it from the inside using Lin's backing, (b) decline formal power and instead
  broker a standing alliance/council among all the factions that answered Ch.1's call,
  effectively founding something new outside the old Bauhinia hierarchy, or (c) walk
  away from politics entirely once the Hollow is dealt with, keeping only the crew and
  the ship — a quieter, more personal ending. Sets flag `secondIgnitionEnding`.
- This choice doesn't change the final battle itself, only the epilogue in Ch.5.

**Characters:** Kade, Yifei Lin, variable crew reactions.

**Choices & Consequences:** Three-way branch as above, resolved in Ch.5's epilogue.

**Systems/Unlocks:** None mechanical.

**Novel grounding:** no confirmed source for the three-path choice structure itself.
第十三卷 帝国风云 ("Imperial Storm," Vol. 13, reached by ch. 448) confirms
political/imperial-power stakes are a real, ongoing thread in the novel's world —
relevant context, not a matched beat. Kept as original invention.

## Chapter 5 — Second Ignition (campaign finale)

**System:** the Umbral Line (core)

**Beats:**
- The full endgame fleet battle against the Hollow, using the complete roster, full
  Sovereign-class fleet, and the coalition assembled in Ch.1 (composition/strength reflecting
  every major flag set across the campaign). Hardest and largest combat in the game,
  multi-phase, deliberately drawing on every enemy doctrine introduced so far as the
  Hollow's presence appears to be warping/echoing them in its final phase.
- Victory doesn't destroy the Hollow outright (kept intentionally non-total, in
  keeping with its cosmic-scale ambiguity) but does drive it back past the threshold it
  came from, sealing the ark's containment function properly for the first time in the
  Mayeth's history — a real, hard-won ending rather than a cure-all.
- **Branching epilogue** (a short sequence of slides/scenes, not more gameplay):
  reflects `secondIgnitionEnding`, `arthaineResolution`, `ridgeReachOutcome`,
  `tigerSharkAlliance`, and which named crew are alive/recruited/lost, giving a
  distinct final tableau per major playthrough shape without requiring separate ending
  "levels."

**Novel grounding:** no confirmed source — the novel has no finale, so nothing to
adapt a campaign-finale beat from (see `docs/story/research-notes-act5.md`). Ch. 120's
"文明吞噬" (Civilization Devouring) is a loose thematic cousin for a draining/
corrupting antagonist concept, but it's chapter 120 of 3000+, not confirmed as any
kind of culminating threat. The Hollow, the non-total-victory ending, and the
branching epilogue are all kept as intentional original invention — the honest
conclusion of real research, not an unexamined default.

**Characters:** Kade, the Cinder, full crew, full coalition.

**Choices & Consequences:** Epilogue slides vary per accumulated flags — see above.

**Systems/Unlocks:** None — **Act V complete, campaign complete.**
