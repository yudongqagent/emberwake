## The novel has no finale to check Act V against

This is a structural fact, not a search failure:

- Faloo's own chapter directory shows status **连载中 (ongoing serialization)**, last
  updated **2026-08-23 — today** —
  [b.faloo.com/look_1_810693_1.html](https://b.faloo.com/look_1_810693_1.html).
- A chapter-list aggregator puts the latest chapter at **#3159, "第三十 应战,"**
  updated 2026-08-23 20:00, tagged 连载 (ongoing) —
  [m.biquting.org/book_19103100.html](https://m.biquting.org/book_19103100.html).
- As of May 2025 the novel was already at 2,756 chapters, also described as still
  serializing —
  [wap.faloo.com/810693.html](https://wap.faloo.com/810693.html),
  [Baidu Baike](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E6%88%91%E7%9A%84%E6%88%98%E8%88%B0%E8%83%BD%E5%8D%87%E7%BA%A7/62057775).
- The manga adaptation is far behind the novel (~155-177 episodes as of mid-2025), so
  it isn't a shortcut to an ending either —
  [baozimh.com](https://www.baozimh.com/comic/wodezhanjiannengshengji-qingtingdongman),
  [colamanga.com](https://www.colamanga.com/manga-fu69264/).

The novel began serialization in 2021 and, roughly five years and 3000+ chapters in
with no announced completion date, is a long-runner still mid-story. There is no 大结局
(grand finale) published anywhere to adapt from.

## What partial resonance exists (all mid-story, none confirmed as endgame)

- **第十三卷 帝国风云 ("Imperial Storm"/Vol. 13)**, reached by ch. 448 as of May 2025 —
  confirms political/imperial-power stakes are a real, ongoing plot thread in the
  novel's world. This is relevant *context* for Act V Ch.4's institutional-power
  choice, but no source describes a three-path branching choice structure anywhere —
  a dedicated search for that came back empty.
- **Ch. 120, "文明吞噬" ("Civilization Devouring")** — thematically adjacent to "the
  Hollow" as a draining/corrupting threat, but this is chapter 120 of 3000+ (very
  early-to-mid story), not confirmed as any kind of culminating antagonist.
- **Ch. 176, "复仇的终结" ("The End of Revenge")** — a loose emotional analog to a
  full-circle payoff beat, but nothing confirms it's a re-fight of the specific
  original death/failure scenario the game's cold open uses. Given the novel's own
  rebirth premise (protagonist knows how he dies, is sent back before it), a full-
  circle rematch is thematically very plausible as something the author is building
  toward eventually — but "plausible eventually" is not "confirmed," and I won't
  claim sourcing I don't have.
- **矿场变故** location name **天顶空洞** ("Zenith Void/Cavity," ch. 59) uses 空洞
  ("hollow/cavity") but as a place name, not an entity — not the same thing as the
  game's Hollow faction, noted only so a future pass doesn't mistake it for a match.

## Conclusion for Act V's design doc

None of Act V's five chapter beats (coalition-rally, the Hollow as final antagonist,
the full-circle re-fight, the three-way power choice, the non-total-victory ending
with branching epilogue) have a confirmed, sourceable novel beat behind them. This is
the honest result of real research, not an assumption — see the 10+ searches above.

Per `docs/content-depth-standards.md` §2's own fallback rule ("if a beat... can't be
traced to something in the research notes, it needs a specific in-world reason for
existing"): Act V's reason is that **the source hasn't gotten there yet**. The
novel's own ongoing-serialization status is itself the honest grounding for treating
Act V as the campaign's most original act — not a shortcut around the research
requirement, but its documented conclusion. `act-5-umbral-line.md` is being annotated
to say this explicitly per chapter, rather than silently presenting invented content
as sourced or force-fitting a weak match (e.g. ch. 120's "文明吞噬" is noted as a loose
thematic cousin, not claimed as the Hollow's source).

If a future pass wants tighter grounding here, the two real options are: (a) revisit
this research once the novel has advanced further / actually concludes an arc that
resembles an ending, or (b) accept Act V as intentionally original, since a five-book
campaign's finale reasonably earns more authorial invention than its tutorial act.
