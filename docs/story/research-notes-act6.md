# Research Notes — Act VI

Required by `docs/content-depth-standards.md` §2. Triggered by player feedback
(2026-08-23): "story runs out at the Umbral Line and then there's nothing more" —
this covers the research for what comes after Act V's ending.

**Source:** 我的战舰能升级 (*My Warship Can Level Up*), by 酣歌 (Han Ge), Faloo.
Research for this pass done 2026-08-23 via 7 additional targeted searches, building
on the negative result already logged in `research-notes-act5.md` (the novel has no
finale — it's still serializing at 3000+ chapters as of this research).

## What's confirmed this pass: a real, specific, previously-untapped arc

Unlike Act V's notes (which found no usable material and said so), this pass found a
genuinely new, well-sourced sequence that Acts I-V never drew on. Multiple
independent chapter-list sources (novel543.com's directory, and cross-checking
search aggregators) agree on this exact sequence and wording:

- **Ch. 375, "群英匯聚" ("Heroes Converge")**
- **Ch. 376, "攬權？我要最高指揮權！" ("Seizing Power? I Want Supreme Command!")**
- **Ch. 377, "父慈子孝？不，是夥伴！" ("Filial Piety? No, We're Partners!")**
- **Ch. 378, "軍事機密！" ("Military Secrets!")**
- **Ch. 379, "戰雲密布之時的大膽之舉！" ("Bold Actions When War Clouds Gather!")**
- **Ch. 380, "戴森球體系！" ("Dyson Sphere System!")**
- **Ch. 381, "福音文明！" ("Gospel Civilization!")**
- **Ch. 382, "文明失格！" ("Civilization Disqualified!")**

This reads as one continuous arc: the protagonist consolidates command authority
during (or after) a coalition-building moment ("Heroes Converge" → "I want supreme
command" → "we're partners, not a hierarchy" → military secrets → a bold wartime
move) — then, immediately following, discovers a Dyson-sphere-scale megastructure
belonging to a named civilization ("Gospel Civilization"), and the arc's very next
chapter title implies that civilization is judged and found wanting ("Civilization
Disqualified").

This is a strong structural match for exactly the situation Act V's ending leaves
Kade in (a coalition just finished consolidating around him, three possible
power-postures chosen at `whatKadeKnows`) followed by a "what's actually out there"
discovery — which is also precisely the hook Act V's closing line ("Let's see what's
past the next horizon, Cinder") sets up.

## What's NOT confirmed — the honest boundary

Chapter *titles* and their sequence are confirmed independently across sources.
Chapter *content* is not — no source accessible to this research gives the actual
prose of what the Dyson sphere contains, who/what the Gospel Civilization is (alive,
extinct, hostile, ascended), or specifically why it's "disqualified" or by whom/what
standard. This is the same shape of result as the Rift-diving research
(`research-notes-extradimensional.md`): a real, confirmed *premise* to build on,
with the specifics being original invention layered on top — not the premise itself
being invented.

Two adjacent findings, checked and ruled out as unrelated:
- The "幽灵战舰" ("Ghost Battleship," ch.131) chapter some searches surfaced sits
  right next to "強化蟲圖騰" (ch.130) — the same early insect-totem material Act II's
  research notes already flagged as North-Territory-adjacent, not later/distinct
  content. Not used here.
- No source connects the Gospel Civilization/Dyson sphere arc (ch.380-382) causally
  to the earlier ancient-civilization atmosphere already used for Act III/IV (the
  Mayeth Constructs, the Hollow) — they're separate confirmed elements from
  different points in the novel, not stated by any source to be the same thread.
  Act VI's design connects them narratively (see below), and that connection is
  original invention, flagged as such.

## Design direction for Act VI

Grounded, per the confirmed sequence: Act VI opens on the *governance* half of the
arc (a coalition that just won a war has to decide what it actually is now — a
direct continuation of Act V Ch.4's three-way choice, "Heroes Converge" /
"supreme command" / "partners, not hierarchy" all mapping cleanly onto that
already-existing branch), then pivots to discovering a dormant Dyson-sphere-scale
megastructure past the Umbral Line, attributed to a named ancient civilization, and
closes on the reveal that this civilization failed some test — original invention:
tied narratively to the Hollow (the same force that "disqualified" the Mayeth
several eras ago), giving Acts IV-VI a connected throughline instead of an unrelated
new threat, while being explicit in-game that this causal link is the adaptation's
own invention, not a sourced novel beat.
