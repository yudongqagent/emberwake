# Act I — Salvage & Survival (Bauhinia Reach)

Tutorial act. Establishes Kade, the Cinder, the core loop (fly, mine/trade/fight,
manage fleet), and the central political conflict with House Arthaine. Ends with a
Cruiser-class refit and the jump lanes out of Bauhinia Reach opening.

Galaxy: **Bauhinia Reach**. Systems used: Amaranth Belt, Bauhinia Prime, Kestrel's
Rest, Thornwake, Coldreach Anchorage.

---

## Chapter 1 — Cold Wake

**System:** Amaranth Belt

**Beats:**
- Cold open: Kade, in the future, dies commanding a flagship in a losing battle — a
  short, wordless, high-intensity combat flash (not playable, a scripted vignette) that
  ends mid-explosion.
- Cut to twenty years earlier: Kade wakes aboard *Whisper*, his old salvage-grade
  corvette (a Corvette-class hull, Salvage rarity, its Aptitude not yet known),
  disoriented. The Cinder introduces itself — calm, precise, oddly evasive about its
  own nature. It grants Scan and Yield immediately.
- Tutorial: free flight controls, approach an asteroid field in Amaranth Belt, mine
  Salvage. Scan a drifting derelict hull fragment — first taste of Aptitude.
- Kade doesn't explain the reincarnation to anyone yet. Internal-monologue framing only
  (the player is inside his knowledge, not broadcasting it).

**Characters:** Kade Ren, the Cinder.

**Choices & Consequences:** None major — this chapter establishes controls and voice.

**Systems/Unlocks:** Free flight, mining interaction, Scan, Yield, Salvage resource.

---

## Chapter 2 — First Blood

**System:** Amaranth Belt → Kestrel's Rest

**Beats:**
- A distress signal POI appears near Kestrel's Rest, a frontier mining outpost. Flying
  to it triggers first combat: a small Shark Reaver raiding party is shaking down the
  outpost.
- Combat tutorial: range bands, weapon firing, basic module use.
- After the fight, dock at Kestrel's Rest. Meet **Ori Vashti**, the outpost's engineer,
  who patched *Whisper* together years ago in the timeline Kade remembers — she doesn't
  know him yet. She's lost a ship of her own to the Reavers before and is done losing
  things quietly. She joins the crew.

**Characters:** Kade, Ori Vashti (recruited).

**Choices & Consequences:** Minor dialogue flavor only.

**Systems/Unlocks:** First combat, first named crew recruit, docking/station
interaction pattern established.

---

## Chapter 3 — The Ledger

**System:** Bauhinia Prime

**Beats:**
- First trip to the capital. Dock at Bauhinia Prime; the Trade screen opens properly
  for the first time (market prices, sell salvage).
- Kade tries to register *Whisper* for expanded docking rights and is stonewalled — a
  minor Arthaine functionary blocks him on a technicality. First on-screen presence of
  House Arthaine's institutional weight, without Sir Arthur himself appearing yet.
- The station's Shipwright's Dock is introduced alongside the Trade screen — Kade's
  first look at drawing additional ship instances, though Source Points are far too
  scarce this early to spend on it yet.
- **Dowager Marchioness Yifei Lin** intervenes, recognizing promise in Kade (or
  remembering a version of him — left ambiguous whether she senses something). Offers
  patronage: she'll sponsor his docking rights and early operations in exchange for
  results against the Reavers, who are hurting her territories' shipping.

**Characters:** Kade, Yifei Lin (introduced, becomes ongoing patron), unnamed Arthaine
functionary.

**Choices & Consequences:** None major yet — sets up the Lin/Arthaine axis that later
choices will play against.

**Systems/Unlocks:** Trade screen, Faction Favor introduced (Bauhinia Principality
track begins, nudged by Lin's patronage).

---

## Chapter 4 — Static and Signal

**System:** Thornwake

**Beats:**
- A Yifei Lin-sponsored survey job sends Kade to Thornwake, a wreck field. Flying in,
  the derelict at the center is unmistakably familiar — it's an echo of the flagship
  Kade died commanding, in an earlier, smaller configuration, decades before its
  future loss. Gut-punch beat: he's looking at his own future grave.
- Investigating the wreck triggers a scripted encounter with residual defense systems
  (not a full combat — a scaled tutorial-style skirmish emphasizing dread over
  difficulty).
- At the wreck's core, the Cinder reacts strangely — a spike of something like
  recognition it won't explain. It unlocks **Lock** here, framed as the Cinder
  "remembering how" rather than simply granting a new power.

**Characters:** Kade, the Cinder.

**Choices & Consequences:** None major — this is a lore/tone chapter.

**Systems/Unlocks:** Lock (module trait selection), Insight resource introduced.

---

## Chapter 5 — Tiger's Reach

**System:** Coldreach Anchorage

**Beats:**
- Intelligence from Ori and Lin's network points to a Shark Reaver forward base at
  Coldreach Anchorage, run by one of Tiger Shark's lieutenants.
- First real boss fight: a fortified enemy formation using the Reaver "fast/evasive/
  boarding" doctrine at real difficulty for the first time.
- Victory yields the act's first meaningful Origin Essence and reveals **Bosun
  "Ratchet" Koi**, a washed-out ex-Principality Navy gunner being held prisoner by the
  Reavers for refusing to fight for them. He joins the crew.
- With enough Origin Essence banked, the Destroyer-class refit becomes available back
  at Bauhinia Prime or any friendly station.

**Characters:** Kade, Ori, Ratchet Koi (recruited), unnamed Reaver lieutenant (defeated,
not killed on-screen — left ambiguous for a possible later callback).

**Choices & Consequences:** None major.

**Systems/Unlocks:** Destroyer-class refit available (6 slots), Origin Essence resource
meaningfully introduced, second named crew recruit.

---

## Chapter 6 — House Rules

**System:** Bauhinia Prime

**Beats:**
- Sir Arthur Arthaine appears in person for the first time — cold, precise, openly
  contemptuous of Kade's rapid rise under Lin's sponsorship. He revokes *Whisper*'s
  docking license on a manufactured technicality, forcing a choice.
- **Choice point:** (a) go through official channels and let Lin fight it politically
  (slower, builds Bauhinia Faction Favor, keeps things clean), (b) pay a bribe through
  an underworld fixer to route around it (faster, costs Salvage, opens a thread with a
  black-market contact useful later), or (c) publicly call out Arthaine's overreach at
  a docking tribunal (risky, damages Arthaine's standing but burns goodwill with
  cautious nobles, unlocks a distinct late-game dialogue option with Seraphine
  Arthaine).
- Whichever path, Kade keeps his docking rights, but the choice sets an early-game flag
  read by several later chapters (notably Act I Ch.6's Seraphine-adjacent beats in Act
  II and the Act IV Arthur confrontation).

**Characters:** Kade, Sir Arthur Arthaine (introduced), Yifei Lin.

**Choices & Consequences:** Three-way branch as above — first major story choice,
sets a persistent flag (`arthaineConflictStyle: political | bribed | public`).

**Systems/Unlocks:** None mechanical — pure narrative branch.

---

## Chapter 7 — Ember Rising (Act I finale)

**System:** Kestrel's Rest

**Beats:**
- Retaliating for Ch.5's loss, the Shark Reavers mass a real assault on Kestrel's Rest
  — Ori's home. Full fleet battle (first multi-wave combat), defending the outpost
  with everything built so far.
- Tiger Shark herself appears in person for the first time (not yet a boss fight — a
  tense stand-off/retreat after the wave is broken, establishing her as a recurring
  threat rather than a one-and-done miniboss).
- Victory banks enough Origin Essence for a Cruiser-class refit. With the Cruiser's
  expanded loadout and the outpost secured, Lin authorizes Kade for the jump lanes out
  of Bauhinia Reach — the galaxy opens.

**Characters:** Kade, Ori, Ratchet, Tiger Shark (seen, not fought to conclusion), Yifei
Lin.

**Choices & Consequences:** Outcome of the defense (how much of the outpost survives
undamaged) is tracked lightly and referenced in flavor text later — not a hard branch.

**Systems/Unlocks:** Cruiser-class refit (8 slots), jump lanes to Lionsheart Expanse
and Swanreach Combine unlocked — **Act I complete.**
