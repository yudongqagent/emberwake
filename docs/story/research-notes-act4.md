# Research Notes — Act IV

Required by `docs/content-depth-standards.md` §2. Like the Act V notes, this file's
main finding is mostly negative — most of Act IV's core beats (the ancient-AI
companion reveal, the containment superweapon, the exposed-betrayal choice structure)
have no confirmed source. Documented in full rather than silently assumed.

**Source:** 我的战舰能升级 (*My Warship Can Level Up*), by 酣歌 (Han Ge), Faloo,
2,756+ chapters as of May 2025, still ongoing. Research gathered 2026-08-23.

## Coverage ceiling

Search/aggregator coverage of this novel is good through roughly chapter 150-200 (the
manga adaptation's current reach) and drops off sharply beyond that — the raw text
past ~ch. 200 exists only on Faloo/fanqienovel/mirrors, not indexed or summarized
anywhere searchable. Act IV's beats, as designed, describe *late*-game material
(the campaign's fourth of five acts), which is almost certainly deep past what any
available source covers. This is a genuine research ceiling, not a shortcut.

One flagged issue: `wtr-lab.com`, which search results claimed hosts 1,786+ raw-
translated chapters, currently redirects to an unrelated gambling site — likely
expired/squatted. Not used as a source.

## What's confirmed

- **The novel's structural conceit is repeated "civilization-of-the-week" contact**:
  "探索无数未知文明，收割文明本源" (explore countless unknown civilizations, harvest
  their origin/source) — confirmed via Baidu Baike and the official synopsis. This
  means encountering a dormant ancient civilization's ruins is a recurring *episodic*
  device in this novel, not a one-off late-game reveal — genre-level support for the
  Mayeth Constructs concept's shape, even without a specific matching arc.
- **Volume 2 (北地风起) chapter titles confirm the pattern in practice** (verified via
  two independent chapter-index mirrors, Tencent Comics and Baozimh): ch. 72
  "强人工智能" (Strong AI), ch. 100 "古代文明遗产" (Ancient Civilization Heritage),
  ch. 141 "深入遗迹" (Venturing Deep into Ruins), ch. 142 "圈养文明" (Caged
  Civilization), ch. 150 "弑神武器" (Godslaying Weapon), ch. 151 "击杀神邸" (Slaying
  the God's Seat). These are early/mid-novel (Volume 2 territory, already the focus of
  a separate Act II research pass) — real material, but the wrong act's timeframe, and
  an *encounter*, not confirmed as a recruited ally or a system-origin reveal.
- **Noble antagonists and betrayal are a real recurring structural element**: 黑石男爵
  (Baron Blackstone, ch. 26, already used for Act I grounding), and a search result
  (unverified directly — source page 403'd, only visible via search snippet) naming
  **石尊侯爵 (Marquis Shi Zun)** as a Volume 3 ("新的启点") antagonist. Separately, a
  chapter titled **ch. 132 "背叛与突变" (Betrayal and Mutation)** was found in the
  Volume 2 index — a real "someone's betrayal" beat exists in the novel, even if not
  confirmed at the specific late-game political-scheming scale Act IV describes.

## What's not confirmed, despite real effort

- **The system's true origin as alien/ancient tech, bonded at the moment of death, with
  its own hidden agenda.** Every synopsis describes 王动's "auxiliary system" (辅助系统)
  identically and neutrally — Scan/source-point-multiplier/trait-select — with zero
  discussion of provenance or a later reveal recontextualizing it. Not ruled out (could
  be deep in unindexed raw text), but not found after real search effort.
- **A recruited AI/robotic ally with its own backstory.** No summary or chapter title
  turned up a companion beyond the system itself; ch. 72's "强人工智能" reads as an
  *encountered*, not recruited, AI — and even that's unconfirmed at the content level,
  title only.
- **A last-resort containment superweapon built by an extinct civilization that
  ultimately lost to a greater threat.** Ch. 150-151's "godslaying weapon" is a loose
  thematic cousin (weapon vs. a god-tier entity) but nothing confirms the specific
  "built as containment, and they lost anyway" framing.
- **A three-way choice structure for resolving an antagonist's exposed corruption**
  (formal exposure / private ultimatum / messy partial-win). No source describes
  anything like this branching-choice shape — reads as original game design.

## Conclusion for Act IV's design doc

Per the same fallback rule used for Act V (`content-depth-standards.md` §2): where a
beat can't be traced to research, it needs a specific in-world reason for existing.
Act IV's honest reason is a **research ceiling** — the act describes late-game
material past what any available source indexes for this still-ongoing, 2700+-chapter
novel. `act-4-deep-origin.md` is annotated per chapter: the general
ancient-civilization-of-the-week *shape* is genre-consistent and worth noting as
context, and the Arthur-betrayal thread gets a loose nod to ch. 132's "背叛与突变" and
the Marquis Shi Zun antagonist pattern — but the AI companion, the system's hidden
origin, the containment superweapon, and the three-way choice structure are all kept
as original invention, clearly marked rather than implied to be sourced.
