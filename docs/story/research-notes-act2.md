# Research Notes — Act II

Required by `docs/content-depth-standards.md` §2. The best-sourced of the four acts
researched this pass (I, III, IV, V already covered) — one strong match (the
recurring-antagonist pattern), one partial match (an insectoid enemy really exists),
and two elements with no confirmed source.

**Source:** 我的战舰能升级 (*My Warship Can Level Up*), by 酣歌 (Han Ge), Faloo,
2,756+ chapters as of May 2025, still ongoing. Research gathered 2026-08-23. Full
chapter-title sequence pulled directly from the manga adaptation's index
([baozimh.com](https://www.baozimh.com/comic/wodezhanjiannengshengji-qingtingdongman),
corroborated by [mkzhan.com](https://www.mkzhan.com/216466/)) — manga episode numbers,
not exact novel chapter numbers (the two drift apart past ~ep.76).

**Volume-numbering caveat**: sources disagree on whether 北地风起 is volume 2 or
volume 4 — flagged, not resolved. The concrete, verifiable unit used here is the
manga's own titled arc (ep. 70-91), regardless of official volume numbering.

## Strong match: the recurring-antagonist-across-territories pattern

**黑石男爵 (Baron Blackstone)**, already used for Act I's grounding, doesn't stay in
one place — his agents follow the protagonist across arcs and territories under
different names: **黑石之虎 (Blackstone's Tiger, ep. 80)** in the North Territory arc,
**黑石之狐 (Blackstone's Fox, ep. 128)** later still. An explicit **复仇之战 (War of
Revenge, ep. 74)** beat confirms this is framed as ongoing, deliberate pursuit, not
coincidence. This is a real, well-sourced pattern: the same antagonist family
resurfacing through a new proxy/agent in each new territory the protagonist enters.

This maps cleanly onto Act II Ch.3 (Blood Debt), where Arthaine resurfaces working
Lionsheart court channels — the game already had this shape by instinct; the research
confirms it's the novel's actual recurring structure, not just a plausible guess.

## Partial match: an insectoid enemy race is real

**虫图腾 (Insect Totem)** first appears at ep. 81 — inside/adjacent to the North
Territory arc — and recurs explicitly at **ep. 129 再遇虫族 ("Re-encounter the Insect
Race")** and **ep. 130 强化虫图腾 ("Strengthen Insect Totem")**. This confirms a
recurring insectoid enemy/civilization is a real element of the novel, consistent
with its core "explore civilizations, harvest their origin" system. What's *not*
confirmed: any "scouts probing/mapping before a full incursion" narrative shape (I
searched specifically for this — 虫巢/母巢/侦察舰/初遇虫族 — and found nothing), and
whether "totem" represents a civilization-scale threat or a single relic/encounter.

Chosen resonance for Act II Ch.6/7: the "totem" framing itself (not the scouts-then-
invasion structure, which stays original).

## No confirmed source

- **Lionsheart Concord (honor-duel culture) vs. Swanreach Combine (trade culture)
  clash.** Searched specifically — the only "duel"-adjacent title anywhere in the
  novel is a single scene (ep. 32, 宴会上的挑战/Challenge at the Banquet, not a
  culture), and the only trade-faction title is a one-off caravan defeat in volume 1
  (already used for Act I's Ch.5 grounding) that never recurs or clashes with
  anything. No ongoing merchant civilization or duel-culture faction exists to clash.
  Kept as original invention — Act II Ch.1, Ch.2, and Ch.5's three-way choice.
- **A hidden pirate shipyard raid.** Pirates recur throughout the novel as an enemy
  type (牙星盗团/White Fang, 巨蜥星盗团/Giant Lizard, 草帽星盗团/Straw Hat), and a
  皇家造船厂 (Royal Shipyard, ep. 39) appears once — but it's allied, not pirate, and
  never raided. No specific shipyard-raid beat exists. Kept as original invention —
  Act II Ch.4.

## Conclusion for Act II's design doc

Ch.3 (Blood Debt) gets real grounding — Arthaine's pattern here matches something
verified as the novel's actual recurring structure, not just genre-plausible guessing.
Ch.6/7 (First Contact / The Reach Opens) get partial grounding — the insect race is
real, but the specific scouts-then-invasion shape and the framing of it as a coming
war are original elaboration. Ch.1, Ch.2, and Ch.4 stay honestly flagged as original
invention with no confirmed source, same treatment as Acts III-V.
