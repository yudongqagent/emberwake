# Research Notes — Act III

Required by `docs/content-depth-standards.md` §2. Mixed result: one genuinely strong,
well-sourced match (the Rift-diving mechanic), and several elements that stay
unconfirmed after real search effort.

**Source:** 我的战舰能升级 (*My Warship Can Level Up*), by 酣歌 (Han Ge), Faloo,
2,756+ chapters as of May 2025, still ongoing. Research gathered 2026-08-23 via 17+
searches; access was heavily obstructed (Baidu Baike/Faloo blocked in-browser, most
aggregators 403'd, one fetch produced a clearly hallucinated wrong-novel title list
that was discarded). Coverage effectively caps around ch. 150-200 of 2756+.

## Strong match: the Rift-diving mechanic is the novel's real core loop

Confirmed independently across every synopsis source (Baidu Baike, Faloo, multiple
mirrors), worded almost identically each time: 王动's special "火种" (Spark-class)
warship can **定期进入异空间，收割源点，强化自身** — "periodically enter alternate/
different space (异空间) to harvest Source Points (源点) and strengthen itself." This
is not a side mechanic — it's the novel's baseline progression loop, present from the
opening volume onward.

Emberwake's Origin Rift / Rift-dive expedition mechanic (introduced this act) is a
genuine, direct adaptation of this — not just thematically similar, but the same
core idea: a special ship type entering unstable pocket-space to harvest the game's
central resource. The one honest caveat: the novel's framing is routine/methodical,
not explicitly framed as dangerous/hostile-territory diving — Act III's "genuinely
dangerous, tone-shift-into-horror" framing is original elaboration on a real
mechanic, not itself sourced.

## Everything else: unconfirmed after real effort

- **Chitin Swarm as an insectoid civilization harvesting the same resource ("dark
  mirror" of the player's economy).** Only a single, low-confidence, uncorroborated
  fragment surfaced (虫图腾/"insect totem" around ch. 81, 再遇虫族/"re-encounter the
  insect clan" ch. 129-130) — and per the separate Act II research pass, that
  material sits inside/near the North Territory arc, not confirmed as a later,
  civilization-scale mirror-economy antagonist. Kept as original invention; see
  Act II's notes for the fuller picture of what's actually there.
- **Tiger Shark reappearing weakened, proposing an alliance.** No source addresses
  Baron Blackstone's (or any pirate leader's) later fate one way or the other — a
  genuine gap in available sources, not a confirmed absence. Kept as original
  invention.
- **A Broodmother boss revealing the Swarm is fleeing something deeper.** Not found.
  Kept as original invention.
- **"Something ancient and deeper is the real threat" reveal.** Genre-plausible and
  thematically supported by real ingredients — a "福音文明" (Gospel Civilization) and
  "戴森球体系" (Dyson-sphere-scale civilization) both appear as confirmed chapter
  titles around ch. 380-382, alongside earlier ancient-ruins content (ch. 100, 141-
  143). These confirm the novel does traffic in ancient-civilization/megastructure
  reveals at real scale. But no source confirms these threads are ever revealed as
  the force *driving* the early pirate/faction conflicts — the specific causal
  reveal Act III's finale uses is original synthesis built on real atmospheric
  elements, not a sourced beat.

## Conclusion for Act III's design doc

Act III is a genuine mixed case, and the design doc says so honestly per chapter:
Chapter 1's Rift-mechanic gets real grounding (and one line sharpened to make the
resonance explicit); the Swarm-as-mirror-economy, Tiger Shark's alliance, the
Broodmother reveal, and the ancient-threat cliffhanger all stay flagged as original
invention, consistent with the same research-ceiling honesty used for Acts IV-V.
