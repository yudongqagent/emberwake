# Act III — Origin Tide (Fractured Veil)

Tone shift toward horror/survival. Deepens the Rift-diving expedition mechanic,
delivers the Tiger Shark redemption arc, and reveals the Swarm is itself a harvester of
Origin Essence — a dark mirror of the player's own economy.

Galaxy: **Fractured Veil**. Systems used: Veil's Edge, the Chrysalis Expanse,
Queenspire.

**Novel grounding:** see `docs/story/research-notes-act3.md`. Mixed result — the
Rift-diving mechanic itself is a genuine, strong match (confirmed across every
synopsis source as the novel's actual core loop: a special ship type periodically
entering alternate/pocket space to harvest the game's central resource). Everything
else this act adds on top of that — the Swarm as mirror-economy antagonist, Tiger
Shark's alliance, the Broodmother, the ancient-threat cliffhanger — stays unconfirmed
after real search effort and is annotated per chapter as original invention.

---

## Chapter 1 — Into the Veil

**System:** Veil's Edge

**Beats:**
- Entry into Swarm territory. Origin Rifts — the pocket-dimension expedition sites
  referenced since the novel-inspired premise — become a real, repeatable System-view
  POI type here for the first time: diving into one is a self-contained short
  expedition (a sequence of small encounters/choices inside a distinct, unstable-
  feeling visual space) yielding high Source Points and a chance at Origin Essence.
- Tone shift sold mechanically: ambient audio gets sparser/tenser, Swarm-controlled
  systems are visibly stripped/hollowed on the Galaxy view.
- First proper Swarm combat at real difficulty (mass + regen doctrine in full).

**Characters:** Kade, current crew.

**Choices & Consequences:** None major.

**Systems/Unlocks:** Rift-dive expedition POI type, first real chance at higher-yield
Origin Essence outside of scripted boss fights.

**Novel grounding:** strong match — this is a direct adaptation of the novel's actual
core progression loop (see act notes above). The Cinder's new line making the
resonance explicit ("this is what Whisper was always built to do") is grounded; the
"genuinely dangerous/hostile territory" framing on top of it is original elaboration,
not itself sourced.

## Chapter 2 — Hive Signal

**System:** the Chrysalis Expanse

**Beats:**
- Investigate a derelict Swarm hive-ship. Environmental storytelling reveals the Swarm
  isn't feral — it's organized around harvesting Origin Essence from Rifts, exactly
  like the player, just at civilizational scale and without Kade's precision (they
  strip Rifts wholesale rather than yielding cleanly).
- First real lore hint that something deeper in the Rifts is provoking this — the
  Swarm is expanding faster and more desperately than its own biology would explain.

**Characters:** Kade, the Cinder (reacts again, still withholding).

**Choices & Consequences:** None major — lore chapter.

**Systems/Unlocks:** None mechanical.

**Novel grounding:** no confirmed source for the Swarm as a civilization-scale
mirror-economy harvester. Only a single, low-confidence, uncorroborated fragment
surfaced for an insect-related enemy elsewhere in the novel — kept as original
invention.

## Chapter 3 — Tiger Shark's Gambit

**System:** Veil's Edge / open Veil space

**Beats:**
- Tiger Shark reappears — cornered, her operation gutted since Act II's shipyard raid,
  now losing ships to Swarm incursions she can't fight alone. She proposes an uneasy,
  transactional alliance: intel and a temporary cease-fire in exchange for help against
  a specific Swarm brood threatening her last real base.
- **Choice point:** (a) accept the alliance and fight alongside her — unlocks her as a
  recruitable crew member later this act, softens future Reaver encounters
  Reach-wide, or (b) refuse and let her handle it alone or finish her off — denies the
  recruit, but grants a one-time Salvage/Alloy windfall from claiming her stores, and
  removes Reavers as a factor for the rest of the game (referenced in Act V's fleet
  count). Sets flag `tigerSharkAlliance: bool`.

**Characters:** Kade, Kessa "Tiger Shark" Vray.

**Choices & Consequences:** Branch as above, read by Ch.5 of this act and by Act V
Ch.1.

**Systems/Unlocks:** Conditional path to fifth named crew recruit.

**Novel grounding:** no confirmed source — no available material addresses Baron
Blackstone's or any pirate leader's later fate. A genuine research gap, not a
confirmed absence; kept as original invention.

## Chapter 4 — The Arthaine Contract

**System:** the Chrysalis Expanse

**Beats:**
- Evidence surfaces (via Priya's trade contacts noticing unusual shipments) that Sir
  Arthur, cornered by his declining political position at home, has opened back-
  channel contact with Swarm-adjacent intermediaries — trying to broker personal
  advantage (technology, or simply information to sell to whoever benefits him) from a
  civilization actively consuming the Reach's border systems. This is the chapter that
  confirms Arthur isn't merely an obstacle, he's actively dangerous.
- No combat — a tense confrontation/interrogation scene with a captured Arthaine
  intermediary. Reveals enough to set up the Act IV confrontation but not enough to
  resolve it here (deliberately withheld: exactly what Arthur is trading and to whom).

**Characters:** Kade, an unnamed Arthaine intermediary, Seraphine Arthaine (if
recruited/relevant — reacts personally to hearing this about her father).

**Choices & Consequences:** None major — sets up Act IV Ch.3.

**Systems/Unlocks:** None mechanical.

**Novel grounding:** same pattern as Act IV Ch.3 (see `docs/story/research-notes-
act4.md`) — Arthur's scheming here is part of the same thread, general
recurring-antagonist-betrayal pattern is real (Baron Blackstone's persistence across
arcs, ch. 132 "背叛与突变"), the specific Swarm-collusion beat is not sourced.

## Chapter 5 — Queenspire

**System:** Queenspire

**Beats:**
- Confront the Swarm Broodmother at Queenspire — the act's major boss fight, hardest
  combat so far, using the full Swarm doctrine at scale (waves of regenerating units
  plus a genuine single hard-hitting Broodmother phase).
- If `tigerSharkAlliance` is true, her ships assist in this battle (flavor + a minor
  mechanical assist) and she formally joins the crew afterward as a recruitable Legend-
  tier crew option.
- Victory doesn't destroy the Swarm as a faction — it breaks the immediate incursion
  and gets a direct answer from the Broodmother (translated/interpreted, not literal
  dialogue): they're fleeing something deeper in the Rifts, not conquering for its own
  sake.

**Characters:** Kade, current crew, Tiger Shark (conditional), the Broodmother.

**Choices & Consequences:** None new — resolves Ch.3's branch mechanically.

**Systems/Unlocks:** Conditional recruitment of Tiger Shark (sixth named crew slot),
major Origin Essence toward a Dreadnought-class refit.

**Novel grounding:** no confirmed source for the Broodmother or her "fleeing
something deeper" reveal. Kept as original invention.

## Chapter 6 — Origin Tide (Act III finale)

**System:** deep within the Fractured Veil (a unique Rift-storm location, not a
standard system)

**Beats:**
- A massive Rift storm event — the deepest Rift dive yet, framed as genuinely
  dangerous rather than routine. Kade and crew push through to find its source: not
  Swarm activity, but something ancient stirring further out, and coordinates to a
  region the Cinder unmistakably reacts to this time — its evasiveness cracks slightly
  for the first time, though it still won't fully explain itself.
- Banks the Origin Essence needed for a Dreadnought-class refit.

**Characters:** Kade, the Cinder, current crew.

**Choices & Consequences:** None major.

**Systems/Unlocks:** Dreadnought-class refit (12 slots), Deep Origin galaxy
coordinates acquired — **Act III complete.**

**Novel grounding:** genre-plausible, thematically supported by real ingredients — a
"福音文明" (Gospel Civilization) and a Dyson-sphere-scale civilization are confirmed
chapter titles around ch. 380-382, alongside earlier ancient-ruins content. But no
source confirms these are ever revealed as the force driving the early antagonists —
the specific causal reveal here is original synthesis on real atmospheric elements,
not a sourced beat.
